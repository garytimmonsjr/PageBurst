# PageBurst Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 8, 2026_

PageBurst is an offline reader for manga, comics and other image-based files that you already have on your device.
This policy explains what the app does and doesn't do with your information.

## The short version

**PageBurst does not collect, store on any server, share or sell any personal data.**
It has no account system, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not request internet access.

## What stays on your device

To work, PageBurst keeps the following **only on your device**, in the app's private storage:

- **Your library list:** series and chapter names, and which files or folders they come from.
- **Reading progress and settings:** last page read, reading mode, and reader preferences.
- **Cover thumbnails** generated from your files.
- **Copies of files you import** with "Import files" or "Copy a folder". Linked folders are read in place and not copied.

None of this is sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it.

## Files and folders

PageBurst can only see the files and folders **you choose** through Android's system file picker.
It doesn't scan your storage and doesn't request broad storage permissions.
When you link a folder, Android grants PageBurst read access to that folder only. You can revoke it anytime by
removing the series in the app or clearing the app's storage.

## Support and links

The **Help & Support** screen can open external links (our Discord server, Telegram or email, if configured).
Tapping one opens that app or website, which has its own privacy policy. Before opening a support chat, PageBurst copies
your app version, phone model and Android version to your clipboard **so you can choose to paste them**.
Nothing is sent automatically.

## Children

PageBurst does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
