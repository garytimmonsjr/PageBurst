# Changelog

## v0.1.0 (beta), first public release

The first beta of PageBurst. Thanks for testing! 🎉

**Library**
- Link a folder to read it in place (no extra storage), or copy folders and files into the app
- Folders become series → chapters automatically, with generated covers
- Table of contents drawer with search, continue-reading card, read/unread tracking
- Rescan linked folders to pick up new chapters (also happens automatically at launch)

**Reader**
- Right-to-left, left-to-right and vertical (webtoon) modes, saved per series
- Two-page spreads in landscape
- Pinch/double-tap zoom, tap zones, page slider, previous/next chapter
- Reader settings: volume-button page turns, brightness, background color, rotation lock, keep screen on

**Formats**
- CBZ/ZIP, CBR/RAR (RAR4), PDF, image-based EPUB, folders of images

**Known limitations**
- RAR5 archives aren't supported yet (re-pack as CBZ)
- No zoom in vertical mode yet
- Text-only EPUBs (novels) aren't supported
