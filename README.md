## Features

**Your library, organized**
- **Link a folder** and PageBurst reads it in place: no copying, no extra storage. New chapters show up automatically.
- Folders become **series → chapters** (`My Series/Chapter 1/…`), with covers generated for you.
- **Table of contents** drawer to jump to any series or chapter, with search.
- Continue-reading card, read/unread tracking, and per-chapter progress.

**A reader that gets out of the way**
- **Right-to-left** (manga), **left-to-right** (comics) and **vertical scroll** (webtoons), saved per series.
- **Two-page spreads** in landscape, paired like a printed book, with wide art kept whole.
- Pinch and double-tap zoom, tap-to-turn, a page slider, and next/previous chapter buttons.
- Volume buttons turn pages, plus app-only brightness, a black/gray/white background and rotation lock.

**Formats:** CBZ/ZIP · CBR/RAR (RAR4) · PDF · image-based EPUB · folders of images (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, AVIF…)

**Private by design:** no account, no ads, no tracking, and no internet access at all. Your files never leave your phone.

> PageBurst is a reader only. It doesn't provide, sell or link to any manga or comics. Bring your own files.

## Install

1. On your Android phone (Android 8.0 or newer), open the **[latest release](../../releases/latest)**.
2. Under **Assets**, tap **`PageBurst-vX.Y.Z.apk`** to download it.
3. Open the downloaded file. If Android asks, allow your browser or file manager to
   **"Install unknown apps"**, then go back and tap **Install**.
4. Open PageBurst, tap **Add manga → Link a folder**, and pick the folder that holds your manga.

**Updating:** download the new APK and install it over the old one. Your library and progress are kept.

<details>
<summary><b>Recommended folder layout</b></summary>

```
📁 Manga                ← link this folder
 ├ 📁 Series One
 │  ├ 📁 Chapter 1      ← a folder of images is a chapter
 │  ├ 📁 Chapter 2
 │  └ 📄 Chapter 3.cbz  ← so is a CBZ / CBR / PDF / EPUB
 └ 📁 Series Two
    └ 📄 Volume 1.epub
```
You can also link a single series folder, or import individual files.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>"App not installed" or a Play Protect warning?</b></summary>

- **Play Protect warning:** Google shows this for apps installed outside the Play Store.
  Tap **More details → Install anyway**. PageBurst requests no permissions beyond the folders you pick.
- **"App not installed":** you probably have a build signed with a different key installed.
  Uninstall the old one first. This only happens with test builds from outside these releases.
</details>
